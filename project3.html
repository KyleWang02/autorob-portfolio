Project 3 · Kyle Wang · EECS 367

Forward kinematics

Disco Point

Robot

A custom URDF arm with three rotating joints—base, shoulder, and elbow—and one vertical sliding joint for a small bounce. The upper arm is 0.65 m long and the forearm is 0.55 m long. A fixed finger makes the pointing direction clear.

Choreography

Ready → high/low × 4 → two fist pumps → final high pose → Ready.

The custom FSM has 25 movement and hold states. Joints move smoothly toward each target within their limits. Transitions wait for the target tolerance and state duration; brief endpoint pauses and a two-second final hold make each pose visible.

Rendering

The video was recorded from make demo with this robot and choreography loaded. The C++ runtime computes forward kinematics. A separate three.js client receives /xform_world through a Python TCP/JSON bridge and draws each link using its column-major world matrix and URDF visual offset. A fixed camera shows the full motion.
